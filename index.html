01 About

Not one profession. One way of thinking.

I am a multidisciplinary person with interests, ambitions and experiences spanning research, public policy, governance, education, data science, technology, beauty, fashion, creative direction and entrepreneurship.

I don't believe my identity should be confined to one discipline. I am intellectually curious, creatively inclined and entrepreneurial in how I think — and deeply interested in understanding how things work, and how they can work better.

I believe there is more to a person than what is immediately visible.

03 Tef Studio

Beauty as a language of identity.

● Founder ● Makeup artistry

Tef Studio is my beauty brand. I founded it and I am the creative force behind it. It grew from one of my earliest and most enduring interests, and from my background in makeup artistry.

My interest goes beyond applying makeup. Beauty is about identity, confidence, individuality and self-expression. I care about beauty experiences that understand different skin tones — particularly the needs and representation of Black women and the wider Black community.

    06 Vision

    A life that holds every part of me.

    I want to build a life and career that make room for my different interests, without giving up one part of myself to develop another.

    I see myself contributing to research, policy, governance and development, while also building creative businesses and exploring opportunities in beauty, fashion and technology. I want to develop professional competence, build sustainable enterprises, create opportunities for others, and contribute to the development of Nigeria and Africa.
